S StudySpark Your notes, turned into practice

A study tool powered by GenAI

Turn your notes into a quiz.

Upload a PDF with selectable text. Gemini creates multiple-choice questions, explains the answers, and points back to the page that supports each one.

PDFs up to 10 MB and 80 pages. Extracted text is sent to Google Gemini and is not saved by this app. Don’t upload confidential files. Scanned image PDFs need OCR.

Reading your PDF and asking Gemini to build the quiz. This can take a little while.
